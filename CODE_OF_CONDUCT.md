# Community conduct

Intentacle welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Discuss the work with respect. Give concrete feedback, credit contributions, and
allow others to correct mistakes without personal attacks.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, sexualized comments, and
repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. Do not publish private information
or use project discussions to target someone elsewhere. These expectations apply
to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces operated for this project.

The maintainer may ask for a change in behavior, remove content, pause a
discussion, or restrict participation according to the seriousness and
repetition of the behavior. Enforcement should focus on keeping participation
safe and fair.

For a conduct concern, open a minimal **Moderation contact requested** issue
without names, accusations, or sensitive details, and ask the maintainer to
arrange a private channel. The initial issue is public. GitHub's own reporting
controls are also available for content or account abuse, including concerns
involving the maintainer. Do not post another person's private information as
part of a report.
